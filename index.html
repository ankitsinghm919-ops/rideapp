How Bambukat works

  1. Find or post

    Riders post a trip. Passengers search by route and date.

  2. Rider approves

    You ask for the seat. The rider says yes or no.

  3. Pay by UPI

    After approval, pay the rider's fuel share directly.

Before you ride

  • Helmet for both

    Every ride says if a spare helmet is carried. If not, bring your own.

  • Contact after approval

    Phone numbers and the map pin stay hidden until the rider approves you.

  • Women-only rides

    Women riders can limit a ride to women passengers, and passengers can filter for those rides.

  • Cost sharing only

    Fares stay near fuel cost, so a ride is not a taxi trip. Pay the rider by UPI after approval.

Distances are straight-line estimates plus 30% for roads. Share your trip details with someone you trust before you ride. Before a public launch, check Motor Vehicles Act rules on paid rides in private vehicles and what a two-wheeler insurance policy covers for a pillion passenger.
