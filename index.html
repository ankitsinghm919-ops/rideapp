Common questions

What is Bambukat?

Bambukat is a bike pooling site for Delhi NCR. A rider with an empty seat posts a trip, and a passenger heading the same way asks for it. The passenger shares the fuel cost, and nobody makes a profit.

Is Bambukat free?

Yes. Posting and requesting rides is free. An optional small support fee can be paid by the rider or the passenger to keep the site running.

How do I pay for a ride?

After the rider approves your request, you pay the rider's fuel share directly by UPI, or as you both agree. Bambukat does not handle that money.

Is it safe to ride with someone new?

Phone numbers and the meeting pin stay hidden until the rider approves you. Riders add a driving licence that shows as a badge, every ride says if a spare helmet is carried, and women riders can offer women-only rides.

Before you ride

  • Helmet for both

    Every ride says if a spare helmet is carried. If not, bring your own.

  • Contact after approval

    Phone numbers and the map pin stay hidden until the rider approves you.

  • Women-only rides

    Women riders can limit a ride to women passengers, and passengers can filter for those rides.

  • Cost sharing only

    Fares stay near fuel cost, so a ride is not a taxi trip. Pay the rider by UPI after approval.

Distances are straight-line estimates plus 30% for roads. Cancellations close 30 minutes before the ride starts, and a reason is shared with the other person. Share your trip details with someone you trust before you ride. Before a public launch, check Motor Vehicles Act rules on paid rides in private vehicles and what a two-wheeler insurance policy covers for a pillion passenger.
